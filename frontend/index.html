About the Reform

Key highlights of the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) tax information

💰

Reduced Tax Rates

Company Income Tax reduced from 30% to 25% for large companies. Small companies with turnover below ₦25 million are now tax-exempt.

🏠

Property Tax Relief

Exemption threshold for primary residence increased to ₦50 million. First-time homebuyers get relief on properties below ₦15 million.

💻

Digital Economy

New provisions for taxing digital services. Non-resident digital service providers must register for VAT in Nigeria.
